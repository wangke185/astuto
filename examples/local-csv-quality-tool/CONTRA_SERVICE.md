## Service description

I will clean, standardize, validate, and deduplicate your CSV or Excel data using a structured Python-based workflow.

This service is designed for operational data, exports, customer lists, research tables, inventory files, reporting inputs, and recurring datasets that contain inconsistent formatting, duplicate records, extra spaces, missing-value markers, inconsistent dates, phone numbers, emails, or other rule-based data issues.

Your original files are preserved. I confirm the cleanup rules before processing and avoid destructive assumptions when the data is ambiguous.

Depending on scope, delivery can include cleaned CSV/XLSX files, validation results, removed-record logs, change logs, summary reports, and a reusable workflow for recurring datasets.

I do not silently perform fuzzy matching, impute missing business data, coerce ambiguous values, or remove records based on guessed business logic.

## FAQ

### Do you use fuzzy deduplication automatically?

No. Fuzzy or entity matching is only used when explicit matching rules are agreed first.

### Will you overwrite my source files?

No. Source files are preserved and cleaned outputs are delivered separately.

### Can you work with recurring datasets?

Yes. For recurring inputs, I can provide a reusable Python workflow and rule configuration as an advanced scope.

### Can you fill in missing business data?

Not by assumption. Missing data is preserved, normalized, flagged, or handled using a rule you provide.
